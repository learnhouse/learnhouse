# Organizations

An organization is the top-level tenant in LearnHouse. Every course, member, setting and piece of content belongs to exactly one organization.

## What an organization has

- **Content**: courses, folders, communities, podcasts, boards and playgrounds.
- **Members**: people with a role in the organization. See [Users and Access](/platform/users).
- **Settings**: name, branding, public menu, landing page, emails, AI and more.
- **A plan** on LearnHouse Cloud, which sets its features and limits.

## Where to manage it

In the dashboard sidebar:

| Area | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | [Settings](/platform/organizations/settings): General, Branding, Menu, [Landing Page](/platform/organizations/landing-page), Course completion, AI, Usage, Other and Danger Zone |
| **Users** | Members, user groups, roles, signups, invites, sign-in methods, two-factor and audit logs |
| **Developers** | [API Access](/developers/api/authentication), [Automations](/platform/automations), [Domains](/platform/organizations/custom-domains), [SEO](/platform/organizations/seo) and [SSO](/enterprise/sso) |

## Getting started checklist

New organizations get a checklist at `/dash/onboarding`, also shown in the dashboard sidebar. It walks you through six steps: create your first course, add content to it, brand your school, open your public site, invite your first learner and open a community. Steps tick themselves off as you visit the matching pages. You can **Skip** a step, or choose **I'll finish later** to hide the checklist. Progress is saved in your browser, so it starts over on another device.
