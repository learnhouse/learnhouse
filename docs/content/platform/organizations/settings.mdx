# Organization Settings

Organization settings live under **Organization** in the dashboard (`/dash/org/settings`). It opens on **Overview**. The tabs are **Overview**, **General**, **Branding**, **Menu**, **AI**, **Other** and **Danger Zone**, plus **Analytics** and **Integrations** on the Enterprise Edition.

<YouTube video="guide-10" />

Changing settings needs a role that can update the organization (the Admin role by default).

<Callout type="info">
Some settings moved. Sign-in methods and two-factor are under **Users** (see [Authentication](/platform/users/authentication)). API access, automations, domains, SEO and SSO are under **Developers** (see [Custom Domains](/platform/organizations/custom-domains) and [SEO](/platform/organizations/seo)). Old links redirect to the new pages.
</Callout>

## Overview

**Overview** is the first tab, and where `/dash/org/settings` opens. It shows your organization at a glance:

- Your logo, name, public address and creation date, with links to the other tabs.
- **Your plan**: the plan name and its status (Active, Free, Trial, Proof of concept, Payment due, Suspended), how it is paid (renewal date and price, or "Billed by contract"), and members and admin seats against their limits. **Manage billing** and **Upgrade** appear on LearnHouse Cloud for people who manage billing.
- **Proof of concept**: during a POC, the days left, the features it unlocks, what happens when it ends and your LearnHouse contact.
- **Usage**: members, admin seats, courses, active members this month, AI credits and newsletter emails against your plan's limits. Bars turn amber near a limit and red at it.
- **Features**: every feature, grouped by area, marked **Included**, **POC**, **Add-on** or **Needs** with the plan that has it.
- **AI credits**: the balance, where the credits went and what each AI feature costs.

Self-hosted instances show the community or Enterprise Edition license instead of a plan. Old links to **Usage** redirect here.

## General

General is one page with five sections. On a computer, a list on the side jumps between them; on a phone, use the **Jump to section** menu. One **Save** bar saves every section at once, and fields with a problem are highlighted before anything is saved.

### Organization

| Field | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organization Name** | 60 characters, required | |
| **Organization Label** | required | A category such as Education, Tech or Business. |
| **Short Description** | 100 characters, required | |
| **About Organization** | 400 characters | |

Your logo is shown with a link to **Branding**, where you change it.

### Contact & links

- **Contact email**: shown to learners who need to reach you. Leave it empty to hide it.
- **Social profiles** (X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) and up to 3 **custom links**, shown in your footer.

### Language & email

| Field | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default Language** | | The language new visitors see and the language of emails sent for your organization. Members can still pick their own interface language. |
| **Email Sender Name** | 64 characters | The display name on emails sent for your organization: sign-in links, password resets, invitations, verification and welcome emails. Only the name changes; the sending address stays the platform's. Leave it empty to use the default ("LearnHouse"). Links and URLs are not allowed. |

### Features

Switch optional features on or off, grouped by area: **Content** (Paths, Library, Podcasts, Boards, Plays), **Engagement** (Communities, Live sessions, Newsletter) and **Selling** (Payments). **Editor** holds **Live collaboration & autosave**, the default for every activity (each activity can override it). A feature that is off disappears from the navigation. Features your plan does not include show **Needs** and the plan, with a link to **Overview** → **Features**. Old links to the **Features** tab open this section.

### Footer

| Field | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Footer Text** | 100 characters | Custom text displayed at the bottom of your organization pages, shown in a preview with your links. |

## Branding

Branding has five sub-tabs. Each shows a live preview of where the asset appears.

### Logos

| Asset | Recommended size | Used on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wide logo** | About 400×200 px, transparent PNG works best | The public header |
| **Square logo** | 512×512 px, no rounded corners | The sign-in panel, the organization switcher, the dashboard sidebar, emails and [certificates](/platform/certifications#what-a-certificate-shows). Until you upload one, the wide logo is used. |
| **Favicon** | 64×64 px | The browser tab |

Logos accept PNG or JPG.

<img src="/img/pages/organizations/org-branding-logos.webp" alt="The Logos sub-tab of Branding, with the wide and square logos and previews of where each one appears" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

### Theme

The theme studio puts every option beside a live preview of a learner page. Switch the preview between desktop and phone, and between the light look and what a visitor in dark mode sees.

- **Presets**: sixteen complete looks (colors, fonts, shapes and layout) in one click. **Reset to default** brings back the original look.
- **Colors**: brand color (header and page tint, email buttons), a secondary color, the accent for buttons and links (neutral, brand or your own), and a warm, cool or neutral tone for the greys. **Import from logo** takes the colors from your logo. Contrast checks (WCAG AA) warn when text would be hard to read.
- **Typography**: font pairings, or any heading and body font from Google Fonts, plus heading size and weight.
- **Shape & depth**: corners, button shape and style, card style, elevation and density.
- **Layout**: header color and layout (standard, centered, minimal), page background (tint, plain, dots, grid, glow), footer style, how pictures sit in cards, and link style.
- **Dark mode**: **Follow the visitor** (the default) shows your public pages in dark mode to visitors who chose it, with your accent adjusted or the dark accent you pick; pages you designed in the page builder keep their own colors unless they have a dark palette. **Always light** shows your light look to everyone.

Changes apply to your public pages after you save.

### Sign-in page

- **Welcome message**: up to 200 characters. The default is "Sign in to access your learning content".
- **Background**: **Gradient** or **Custom** (upload a PNG, JPG or WebP image, or generate one with AI).
- **Text color**: **Light** or **Dark**, to stay readable on your background.

### Social & links

- **Social profiles**: links to your X (Twitter), Facebook, Instagram and YouTube pages, shown in the footer.
- **Custom links**: up to 3 extra footer links, each with a label and a URL.

### Previews

- **Thumbnail**: 1200×600 px. Used on the explore page and in link previews.
- **Preview gallery**: up to 4 images or YouTube/Loom videos that showcase your organization. Drag to reorder.

## Menu

**Menu** controls the links in your public navigation. You can switch each built-in link on or off, rename it and drag it into a new order: **Courses**, **Paths**, **Library**, **Podcasts**, **Live**, **Communities**, **Playgrounds** and **Store**.

Use **Add a custom link** to add your own, with a label, a URL and an icon.

<img src="/img/pages/organizations/org-menu.webp" alt="The Menu tab with the custom link form and a switch for each built-in link" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

A link only appears when its feature is on your plan and enabled. The editor marks those that are not with "Requires ... plan" or "Feature is off". Hidden pages also lose their [keyboard shortcut](/platform/search/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Landing Page

Your home page and extra pages, designed in the page builder. See [Landing Page](/platform/organizations/landing-page).

## Course completion

This sets the screen learners see after they finish a course. A live **Preview** updates as you type.

| Field | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Message** | 500 characters | Replaces the default line under the congratulations heading. |
| **Button text** | 60 characters | Defaults to "Browse all courses". |
| **Button link** | | A path on your site starting with `/`, or a full `http://` or `https://` URL. Leave it empty to send learners to the course catalog. |

## AI

Turn **Enable AI Features** on or off for the whole organization, and switch **AI Copilot** on or off. **AI Copilot** controls Copilot and the other [agents](/platform/messages#agents) in Messages. Editor assistance and AI learning activities follow the main switch. Only admins can change these. See [AI](/platform/ai) for what each feature does.

On LearnHouse Cloud, the AI tab is shown from the Standard plan up.

## Other

### "Made with LearnHouse" watermark

A switch controls the "Made with LearnHouse" badge on your public pages and the "Powered by LearnHouse" line in emails.

| Deployment | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| LearnHouse Cloud, free plan | Always shown. The switch is disabled. |
| LearnHouse Cloud, paid plans | You choose. |
| Enterprise Edition | Hidden by your license. |
| Open source | The page badge follows the switch. Emails always show the line. |

### Scripts

Add custom JavaScript to your organization's pages, for example an analytics or chat widget. Each script has a **Script Name** and **Script Content**. Click **Add Script** to save it.

<Callout type="warning">
Scripts run on your pages with full access to them. Only add code you trust.
</Callout>

## Danger Zone

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove all members** | Removes everyone except you. Content is kept. |
| **Wipe all content** | Deletes all courses and their content. The organization and its members are kept. |
| **Delete this organization** | Deletes the organization. Type the organization slug to confirm. |

None of these can be undone.
