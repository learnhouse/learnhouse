# Custom Domains

Serve your organization from your own domain, such as `learn.mycompany.com`, instead of the default LearnHouse address.

<Callout type="info">
Custom domains are shown from the **Pro** plan on LearnHouse Cloud. Changing them needs a role that can update the organization.
</Callout>

## Adding a domain

<Steps>

### Add the domain

Go to **Developers** → **Domains** and click **Add Domain**. Enter a domain or subdomain, for example `learn.mycompany.com` or `academy.example.org`. If you paste a full URL, the protocol and path are removed.

<img src="/img/pages/organizations/custom-domain-add.webp" alt="The Add Custom Domain dialog on the Domains tab, with learn.kitebase.dev entered" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

### Create the DNS records

LearnHouse shows two records under **DNS Configuration Required**. Add both at your domain registrar or DNS provider (Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Namecheap and so on):

| Record | Purpose | Host / Name | Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **TXT Record** | For verification | `_learnhouse-verification` (or `_learnhouse-verification.<subdomain>` for a subdomain) | `learnhouse-verify=<token>` |
| **CNAME Record** | For routing | Your domain or subdomain | `<your-org-slug>.<platform domain>`, for example `myschool.learnhouse.io` on LearnHouse Cloud |

Use the copy buttons to avoid typos.

### Verify

Click **Verify DNS**. LearnHouse looks up the TXT record. Once it is found, the domain becomes **Verified**. DNS changes may take up to 48 hours to spread, but they usually show up within minutes. If verification fails, the domain stays **Pending** and LearnHouse tells you what it could not find.

### Check HTTPS

After verification, the **SSL Certificate** column shows the HTTPS status: **SSL Active**, provisioning, or a problem with the certificate. Click **Check** to refresh it.

</Steps>

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Waiting for the TXT record |
| **Verified** / **Active** | The TXT record was found and the domain serves your organization |
| **Failed** | Verification failed |

To stop using a domain, click **Remove**. This cannot be undone.

## Good to know

- Sessions started on your custom domain are always tied to your organization. See [Authentication](/platform/users/authentication#sharing-sessions-with-learnhouseio).
- If another organization added the same domain but never verified it, you can still claim it by completing verification.
- On a self-hosted instance, the CNAME target uses your `LEARNHOUSE_DOMAIN`. See [Environment Variables](/self-hosting/configuration/environment-variables).
