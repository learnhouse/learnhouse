# Landing Page

Your organization's home page is a page you design in the **page builder**: you stack sections, edit text in place, and style everything for desktop, tablet and phone. Course lists, pricing, paths, communities and forms pull in your live content.

<YouTube video="growth-2" />

Go to **Website** in the sidebar. It shows what visitors see right now, whether the home page is live, your pages, your theme and the template gallery. Click **Open the builder** to edit. (The old **Organization** → **Landing Page** link opens it too.)

<Callout type="info">
Until you publish your own home page, visitors see a default page built from your organization: a short welcome and your course catalog. Organizations that had a landing page from the previous editor were converted automatically, sections, colors and backgrounds included.
</Callout>

## Starting points

- **Templates**: fifteen designed templates, one per kind of school (academy, bootcamp, corporate training, university, creator and coach, language school, health and CE credits, coding school, kids, nonprofit, cohort course, membership community, partner academy, art and design school, certification body), plus the Simple catalog. Each has its own colors, fonts and layout, real copy with your organization's name, and live sections that show your own courses, paths, live sessions and communities. Preview a template full size on desktop or phone, in its own colors or in **My brand** (your brand color and fonts), then use it on the home page or a new page. Your current draft goes to the history first.
- **Blank page**: start empty and add sections from the library.
- **Write with AI**: describe your organization and the page you want, and AI drafts it for you to review.

## The builder

- **Add** (left panel): ready-made sections (hero, courses, pricing, testimonials, FAQ, navbar, footer and more) and single elements (heading, text, button, image, video, form, countdown…). Click to add, or drag onto the page. A navbar always goes to the top and a footer to the bottom.
- **Designed** (left panel): every section of the fifteen templates, to add one at a time.
- **Saved**: sections you saved with the bookmark in the inspector, ready to add to any page of your site.
- **Layers**: the page as a tree. Drag to reorder, click to select.
- **Inspector** (right panel): the selected element's **Content**, **Style** and **Settings**. With nothing selected it shows the page **Theme**.
- **Breakpoints**: switch between desktop, tablet and phone at the top. A style change made on tablet or phone applies to that size and smaller only.
- Double-click a heading, text or button to edit its text in place. Undo and redo work as usual (Cmd/Ctrl+Z).

## Theme and fonts

The theme sets the page colors, corner radius, button style, width and spacing. **Brand** values follow your organization's branding.

Pick a **heading font** and a **body font** from the curated list. Each name is shown in its own font, and **Font pairings** set both at once. **System** fonts use the visitor's own fonts and load instantly. Any element can use its own font from **Style** → **Typography**. Fonts are only downloaded when the page uses them.

**Use my organization's theme** sets the page's colors and fonts to follow your branding, so a later change in **Branding** → **Theme** updates the page too.

The page shows in the colors you chose, whatever the visitor's light or dark mode setting. To follow visitors in dark mode, turn on a **Dark palette** (page theme → Dark mode) and pick its colors. If your organization keeps its public pages **Always light** (Branding → Theme → Dark mode), the dark palette is not used.

## Options on every element

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Visibility | **Everyone**, **Signed-in users only** or **Signed-out visitors only** |
| Hide on | Desktop, tablet or phone |
| Schedule | Show from / until a date and time, handy for launches and promotions |
| Animation | Fade or slide in as it scrolls into view |
| Anchor | An id to link to, like `#pricing` |

## Search and sharing

In the page theme, set the **page title**, **description** and a **social image** for links shared on social networks and chat apps. **Hide from search engines** keeps a page out of search results.

## Saving, history and publishing

Changes save automatically as a draft. **Preview** opens the draft in a new tab with a private link. **Publish** shows what changed since the live version and puts the draft live. **History** keeps every publish, autosave checkpoint and AI edit, and can put any of them back into the draft. If the page is edited in another tab or by Atlas while you work, the builder tells you instead of overwriting.

## Extra pages and custom code

- **Extra pages** such as `/p/about` are included from the **Standard** plan (up to 20). A new page starts with the home page's navigation and footer, and can be added to the home page's navigation in one click.
- The **Custom code** element (your own HTML and CSS, shown in a sandbox) is included from the **Pro** plan.

## Atlas, MCP and the API

Atlas can design and edit pages for you: ask it to build a launch page or to make the hero warmer. Its edits land in the draft with a review bar, and it only publishes when you approve. The same landing tools are available to AI apps over MCP (`?toolsets=landing`) and to API tokens, including listing and applying templates, adding a template section, and reading or changing the organization theme (`get_org_theme`, `update_org_theme`). Theme changes go live on every public page at once, so they always need your approval.
