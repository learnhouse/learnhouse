# Podcasts

Podcasts let you publish audio shows inside your organization. Each podcast holds a list of episodes that learners can stream, and every podcast has an RSS feed you can submit to podcast directories.

<YouTube video="guide-9" />

<Callout>
Podcasts must be enabled in your [Organization Settings](/platform/organizations/settings). On LearnHouse Cloud they need the Standard plan or higher.
</Callout>

## Creating a podcast

Go to **Podcasts** in the dashboard and create a podcast. Give it a **Podcast Name** (3 to 100 characters), an optional **Description** (up to 500 characters), and choose **Make this podcast public**.

Each podcast then has three tabs: **General**, **Episodes** and **Distribution**.

## General

| Setting | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Thumbnail** | The podcast artwork. JPG or PNG, 1400x1400 pixels recommended. You can also generate one with AI. |
| **About** | A longer presentation, up to 2000 characters. |
| **Tags** | Comma-separated keywords. |
| **Visibility Settings** | **Public**: anyone can find and listen, even without an account. **Published**: the podcast shows on your organization's Podcasts page. |

Click **Save Changes**. **View Podcast** opens the page learners see.

<img src="/img/pages/podcasts/podcast-general.webp" alt="The General tab of a podcast, with description, about, tags and the Public and Published checkboxes" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

## Episodes

Click **New Episode** (or **Create First Episode** on a new podcast) and give it a **Title**, a **Description** and an **Audio File**: MP3, WAV, OGG or M4A, up to 1 GB. Click **Create Episode**. The episode starts as **Unpublished**, so nobody hears it before you are ready.

Hover an episode and press play to check the audio, then open its menu and choose **Publish**. Each episode gets the next number. Drag episodes to reorder them. From the menu you can also **Edit** an episode to fix its title and notes or replace its audio file, unpublish it, or delete it. Only published episodes are visible to learners and included in the feed.

<img src="/img/pages/podcasts/podcast-episodes.webp" alt="The Episodes tab with three numbered, published episodes" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

## Listening

Learners find every published podcast under **Podcasts** in the top menu. A podcast's page lists its episodes with their number, notes and length, next to the host and tags. One click on an episode starts it in a player at the bottom of the screen.

Expand the player for the full waveform, 15-second skips back and forward, and the volume. The player keeps playing while learners move around the site, and closes with its cross. Press `p` to play or pause it.

<img src="/img/pages/podcasts/podcast-player.webp" alt="A podcast page with an episode playing in the expanded player at the bottom of the screen" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

## Distribution

Every podcast has an RSS feed at `/podcast/{id}/feed` on your organization's domain, where `{id}` is the podcast UUID without its `podcast_` prefix. The **Distribution** tab shows the feed URL with copy and open buttons, and step-by-step instructions to submit it to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube Music and Amazon Music / Audible.

<img src="/img/pages/podcasts/podcast-distribution.webp" alt="The Distribution tab with the RSS feed URL and submission steps for Apple Podcasts and Spotify" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

**Preview** opens the feed itself. The tab also has tips before you submit: a cover of at least 1400x1400 pixels, a clear description, and at least one published episode.

LearnHouse does not submit the feed for you. You submit it once, then each directory reads new episodes from the feed on its own schedule. The feed is cached for up to an hour, so a new episode can take that long to appear in it.

## Podcasts in courses

The [Audio block](/platform/editor/blocks#the-audio-block) in a course page can embed a single episode (**Episode** tab) or a whole podcast as a playlist (**Playlist** tab). A playlist stays in sync with the podcast, so new episodes appear in the lesson by themselves, and it moves on to the next episode when one ends. Podcast episodes can also be added to [boards](/platform/boards).

<img src="/img/pages/podcasts/podcast-audio-blocks.webp" alt="A lesson with a single-episode Audio block and a playlist of a whole podcast" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

To give a podcast its own step in a course, add a **Resource** activity, open the **Podcasts** tab and add the podcast. Learners then open the podcast inside the course. You can also add podcasts to your [Library](/platform/courses/library) next to your courses.

<img src="/img/pages/podcasts/podcast-resource-activity.webp" alt="The Create Activity dialog for a Resource activity, with the Podcasts tab listing the organization's podcasts" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

A podcast can also be sold as a [product](/platform/payments/offers). Until someone buys it, the episode list stays visible as a preview and the audio is locked. New episodes also appear on your members' [Calendar](/platform/calendar) on the day they are published.

<Callout type="info">
AI audio generation (text to speech, two-voice podcast, single-voice talk) lives in the Audio block of a course page, not in the podcast dashboard. See [AI for Teachers](/platform/ai/for-teachers#audio-generation).
</Callout>
