# Trails

A Trail is a learner's record of progress across the courses they have started. Each learner has one per organization.

## How progress is recorded

- When a learner starts a course, it is added to their Trail.
- Each activity they complete is recorded. Learners mark an activity complete with **Mark as complete** (or the `m` key). Assignments are completed when they are submitted with automatic grading, or when a teacher clicks **Finalize & Complete**.
- A course is finished when every activity is complete. If the course has a [certification](/platform/certifications), the certificate is awarded at that point, provided every graded assignment is passed.

Learners can undo a completion with **Unmark Activity**, which lowers their progress.

## The Progress page

Learners open their Trail from the **Progress** page (`g` then `t`). It shows:

- Every course in progress, with how far along they are.
- **My Certificates**: the certificates they have earned.

<img src="/img/pages/courses/progress-page.webp" alt="The Progress page with course cards showing the percentage completed, a finished course with its certificate, and the Quit All Courses button" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

**Quit Course** on a course card removes that course and its progress from the Trail. **Quit All Courses** on the Progress page does the same for every course.

<Callout type="warning">
Quitting a course deletes the learner's progress in it. It can't be undone.
</Callout>
