# Courses

Courses are the core content unit in LearnHouse. A course is made of chapters, and each chapter holds activities that learners work through in order.

## Creating a course

Go to **Courses** in the dashboard and click **Create Course**. You get three choices:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Start from Scratch** | Fill in a short form and start with an empty course. |
| **Start with AI** | Describe the course and let AI plan the chapters and write the activities. See [AI Course Creator](/platform/courses/ai-course-creator). |
| **From Existing Content** | Upload your videos, PDFs, images and audio and organize them into a course. See [Import & Export](/platform/courses/import-export#from-existing-content). |

The **Start from Scratch** form asks for:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Course Name** | Required, up to 100 characters. |
| **Description** | Required, up to 1000 characters. |
| **Course Thumbnail** | **Upload Image** or **Paste from Clipboard**. |
| **Course Learnings** | What learners will get out of the course. Press Enter to add each one. |
| **Course Tags** | Keywords for the course. |
| **Course Visibility** | **Public** (visible on the internet) or **Private** (signed-in users only). |

A first chapter is created for you. To bring in a course from another platform, use **Import Course** instead. See [Import & Export](/platform/courses/import-export).

<Callout>
On the Free plan of LearnHouse Cloud an organization can have one course. Other plans and self-hosted instances have no course limit.
</Callout>

## Managing courses

The course list in the dashboard lets you:

- Search courses and filter them by user group.
- Click **Published** / **Unpublished** on a course to toggle whether learners can see it.
- **Clone Course**: make a full copy, created as private.
- **Export Course**: download the course as a `.zip` package.
- **Delete Course**.

Click **Select** to act on several courses at once: **Clone Selected**, **Export Selected** (up to 20 courses per export) or **Delete Selected**.

## Course settings

Open a course from the dashboard to edit it. The settings are split into tabs: **General**, **Content**, **Access**, **Contributors**, **SEO**, **Certification** and **Analytics**. See [Course Settings](/platform/courses/settings) and [Chapters & Activities](/platform/courses/chapters-and-activities).
