# Taking a Course

This page describes what learners see when they follow a course.

<YouTube video="guide-4" />

## The course page

The course page shows the description, what learners will get out of it, the authors, any [course updates](/platform/courses/settings#course-updates), and the list of chapters and activities. If a [community](/platform/discussions) is linked to the course, its latest threads appear under **Community Discussions**.

Click **Start Course** to open the first activity. Once a learner has started, the button continues where they left off.

<img src="/img/pages/courses/course-page.webp" alt="A public course page with the cover, description, the Start Course button, the author and Course Updates" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

If the course uses [drip content](/platform/courses/drip-content), a banner names the next chapter and when it opens, upcoming chapters show their opening date, and **Add to calendar** downloads every release as an `.ics` file.

## Inside an activity

<img src="/img/pages/courses/activity-page.webp" alt="A lesson seen by a learner, with the course progress bar, Ask AI, Chapters, the Previous and Next buttons and the Complete status" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Previous activity** / **Next activity** | Move through the course in order. |
| **Mark as complete** | Records the activity as done in the learner's [Trail](/platform/courses/trails). Clicking it again offers **Unmark Activity**. |
| **Focus Mode** | Hides the course navigation so only the content is on screen. |
| **Ask AI** | Opens an AI chat about the current activity. See [AI for Students](/platform/ai/for-students). |
| **Share** | **Copy Link** to the activity, or **Embed** to copy an `<iframe>` code that shows the activity on another site. |

Locked chapters and activities show **This activity is locked** with a hint to sign in or join the right user group. See [Locking chapters and activities](/platform/courses/chapters-and-activities#locking-chapters-and-activities).

### Videos

Video activities open on a dark band across the page, so the video stands out. Uploaded videos play in the LearnHouse player (built on Video.js), which has:

- Adaptive streaming with a quality menu, and preview thumbnails when you hover the timeline, for videos that are streamed in several qualities.
- Playback speed, picture-in-picture, AirPlay and fullscreen.
- Subtitles in several languages. See [AI Video Captions](/platform/courses/video-captions).
- Chapters, drawn as segments on the timeline and listed under the player under **Chapters**, so learners can jump to a section.

When the video is paused, the previous and next activities appear on either side of the player, so learners can move on without leaving it.

<img src="/img/pages/courses/video-player-paused.webp" alt="A paused video activity on the dark band, with the video title and the previous and next activities shown on either side" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

YouTube videos play in the YouTube player.

### End of the course

After the last activity, learners reach the end screen. If the course has a certification and they earned it, they can download it as a PDF and open its verification page. See [Certifications](/platform/certifications).

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` anywhere to see the shortcuts available on the current page. On a course page, `s` starts the course (or continues where you left off) and `e` expands or collapses all chapters. The full list, including the command palette and the `g` navigation shortcuts, is in [Command Palette and Keyboard Shortcuts](/platform/search/keyboard-shortcuts).

### In an activity

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `j` or `→` | Next lesson |
| `k` or `←` | Previous lesson |
| `]` / `[` | Next / previous chapter |
| `m` | Mark lesson complete |
| `f` | Toggle focus mode |
| `Esc` | Exit focus mode |
| `Space` | Play or pause the video |
| `c` | Back to the course page |

In right-to-left languages the arrow keys are swapped. Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a text field.

Search (`/` or `Cmd+K`) finds lessons as well as courses. See [Search](/platform/search).
