# AI Course Creator

The **AI Course Creator** plans a course with you in a chat, then writes the content of each activity. Open it from **Courses** in the dashboard: click **New Course**, then **Start with AI**.

<YouTube video="guide-6" />

<Steps>

### Describe the course

In the chat, describe the course you want: topic, audience and goals. You can also start from one of the templates (**Web Development**, **Data Science**, **Marketing**, **Language**, **Business**, **Design**).

<img src="/img/pages/courses/ai-creator-start.webp" alt="The AI Course Creator before the first message, with the Plan and Content tabs, the template suggestions and the chat box with Attach and Send" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

Use **Attach** to give the AI source material:

| Attachment | Accepts |
| --- | --- |
| **Photo** | PNG, JPG or GIF images. |
| **YouTube Video** | A YouTube URL. The AI analyzes the video, which can take 1 to 2 minutes. |
| **Document** | PDF, DOC or TXT files. |

### Review the plan

The AI proposes chapters and activities in the **Plan** tab. Keep chatting to ask for changes, or edit the plan yourself with **Add Chapter** and **Add Activity**. You can ask for changes up to 10 times. After that you can still edit the plan by hand.

<img src="/img/pages/courses/ai-creator-plan.webp" alt="A proposed plan in the Plan tab: the course title, description and learnings, and three chapters with two activities each" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

### Create the course

Click **Create Course**. LearnHouse creates the course with its chapters and activities. The course starts private and unpublished, and its activities start as drafts.

### Generate the content

In the **Content** tab, click **Generate** on an activity to have its page written, or **Generate All** for every activity. When everything is generated, click **Open in Editor** to review and publish the course.

<img src="/img/pages/courses/ai-creator-content.webp" alt="The Content tab with every activity marked Done, 7 of 7 generated, and the Open in Editor button" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

</Steps>

## AI credits

Starting the plan, each change to the plan, and each generated activity use [AI credits](/platform/ai#ai-credits): 1 credit each, or 3 credits each when your organization uses the larger "pro" model (the Pro and Enterprise plans on LearnHouse Cloud). Creating the course from the plan is free.

<Callout>
Every activity the AI Course Creator makes is a Dynamic Page. You can add videos, assignments and other activity types afterwards. Review what it writes before publishing, especially facts, figures and code.
</Callout>
