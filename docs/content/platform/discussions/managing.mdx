# Managing Communities

Open a community from **Communities** in the dashboard to change its settings.

## General

**Community Name** (3 to 100 characters), **Description** (up to 500 characters) and **Public Community**.

## Thumbnail

Upload a cover image: JPG, JPEG or PNG, up to 8 MB. A 16:9 image works best.

## Access

| Mode | Who can see and post |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone can view and participate. |
| **Restricted** | Only members of the user groups you link. |

## Linked Course

Connect the community to a course so its threads show on the course page. See [Linking a community to a course](/platform/discussions#linking-a-community-to-a-course).

## Moderation

The **Moderation** tab sets rules that discussions and replies are checked against. Posts that break a rule are rejected with an error and never published.

### Content restrictions

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Block external links** | Reject discussions or replies that contain URLs. |
| **Disable reactions** | Turn off emoji reactions in this community. |
| **Allow rich content** | Let members embed YouTube videos in their discussions. Only YouTube is supported. Off by default. |

### Length limits

**Minimum post length**, **Maximum post length** and **Maximum reply length**, in characters. Leave at 0 to disable.

### Rate limits

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Slow mode (seconds)** | Minimum wait between posts from the same user. |
| **Max posts per user per day** | Daily cap per user. |
| **Auto-lock stale threads (days)** | Lock discussions after this many days without activity. |

Set any of them to 0 to disable it.

### Account requirements

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum account age (days)** | Users whose accounts are newer cannot post. |
| **Require verified email** | Only users with a verified email address can post. |

### Blocked words

Add words or phrases one at a time with **Add blocked word**, or several at once with **Batch add words** (comma separated). Discussions and replies that contain a blocked word are rejected.

<Callout>
Rules are checked when a discussion or reply is posted or edited. Changing the rules does not re-check posts that already exist.
</Callout>
