# Discussions & Replies

## Starting a discussion

Click **New Discussion** in a community. The **Start a Discussion** form has:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | One of the labels below. |
| **Discussion Title** | Required, 5 to 200 characters. You can add a custom emoji next to it. |
| **Details (optional)** | Formatted text: bold, italic, lists and more. If the community allows rich content, you can also embed YouTube videos. |

Then click **Post Discussion**.

### Labels

| Label | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Anything. This is the default. |
| **Question** | Questions and answers. |
| **Idea** | Suggestions. |
| **Announcement** | News from the team. |
| **Showcase** | Sharing work. |

Members can filter a community's discussions by label.

### Editing

Authors can edit their own discussion up to 2 times. After that it can no longer be edited by its author. Moderators can edit without a limit.

## Replies

Write in the **Write a reply...** box and click **Reply** (or press `Cmd+Enter` / `Ctrl+Enter`). Replies are listed in order under the discussion. Authors can edit and delete their own replies.

A locked discussion shows **This discussion is locked** and accepts no new replies.

## Upvotes

Members can upvote discussions and replies. Upvotes drive the **Top** and **Hot** sorting:

- **Latest activity**: newest first.
- **Top**: most upvoted first.
- **Hot**: a mix of upvotes and age, so recent popular threads rise to the top.

## Reactions

Members can react to a discussion with emoji: 👍 ❤️ 🎉 🚀 👀 💯 🔥 💡 👏 🙌. Each member can add each emoji once, and hovering a reaction shows who reacted. Reactions can be turned off per community in its moderation settings.

## Moderation actions

Organization admins and users allowed to manage the community can act on any discussion:

- **Pin discussion** / **Unpin discussion**: keep it at the top of the list.
- **Lock discussion** / **Unlock discussion**: stop or allow new replies.
- **Delete discussion**, or delete any reply.
- **Select** several discussions in the list and delete them together.

For automatic rules (blocked words, rate limits, auto-locking), see [Managing Communities](/platform/discussions/managing#moderation).
