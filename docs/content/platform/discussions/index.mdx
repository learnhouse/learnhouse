# Communities

A community is a discussion space inside your organization. Members start discussions, reply, upvote and react. You can have as many communities as you need: one for the whole organization, one per course, one per cohort.

## Creating a community

Go to **Communities** in the dashboard and click **New Community**. Give it a name (3 to 100 characters), an optional description (up to 500 characters) and choose whether it is public.

Each community then has its own settings, with tabs for **General**, **Thumbnail**, **Access**, **Linked Course** and **Moderation**. See [Managing Communities](/platform/discussions/managing).

A community can also be sold, alone or with other content, as a [product](/platform/payments/offers). Until someone buys it, they see the community's page but not its discussions.

## Linking a community to a course

In the **Linked Course** tab, click **Link Course** and pick a course. The course page then shows the community's latest threads under **Community Discussions**, with **View All** and **Start the first discussion** links. A community can be linked to one course at a time. Use **Unlink** to remove the link.

## How members use it

Members find communities on the organization's **Communities** page (`g` then `m`). Inside a community they can:

- Start a discussion with **New Discussion** (or the `n` key).
- Search discussions, filter them by label, and sort by **Latest activity**, **Top** or **Hot**.
- Open a discussion to reply, upvote and react.

<img src="/img/pages/communities/community-page.webp" alt="A community page with its labelled discussions, upvote counts, search, label filter and sorting, and the linked course in the sidebar" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

Users who are not members of the organization can read public communities but are asked to join before they can reply. See [Discussions & Replies](/platform/discussions/comments).
