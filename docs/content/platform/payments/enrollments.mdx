# Store & Checkout

## The store

Your organization's store lives at `/store`. It lists your public offers with their price, type and benefits, and each offer has its own page at `/store/offers/<offer id>` showing what it includes. The store is public and can be indexed by search engines.

Offers also appear on the pages of the courses they unlock. A paid lesson shows "Paid Content" with a **Get Access** button that leads to the offer.

If payments is turned off for the organization, the store shows "Store not available".

## Checkout

1. The learner opens an offer and clicks **Get access** (one-time) or **Subscribe now** (subscription). They must be signed in; otherwise they are sent to the login page first and brought back.
2. LearnHouse opens a Stripe Checkout page.
3. When Stripe confirms the payment, the learner gets access to the offer's courses and payment group right away.

## Enrollment statuses

Each purchase creates an enrollment that tracks the learner's access:

| Status | Meaning | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Checkout started, payment not confirmed yet | No |
| **Completed** | One-time payment succeeded | Yes |
| **Active** | Subscription is paid and running | Yes |
| **Failed** | The payment failed | No |
| **Cancelled** | The subscription ended or a payment was disputed | Removed |
| **Refunded** | The payment was fully refunded in Stripe | Removed |

Statuses update automatically from Stripe webhooks. The **Customers** tab under **Payments** → **Overview** lists every enrollment. Charges and subscriptions there can also show Stripe's own statuses, such as **Paid**, **Trialing** or **Past due**.

## Learners' purchases and billing

Learners find what they bought under their account settings, in **Purchases**. From there, **Invoices & receipts** opens the Stripe billing portal to view and download invoices. What else learners can do in the portal, such as updating a card or cancelling a subscription, follows your Stripe customer portal settings.

<Callout type="info">
LearnHouse does not support coupon or promo codes for course sales. Use **Customer Choice** pricing on one-time offers if you want flexible prices.
</Callout>
