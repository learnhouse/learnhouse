# Customers & Access

The **Customers** tab lists everyone who has, had, or has been invited to access, whatever way they got it.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Has access | Paid, claimed, or was granted access. |
| Invited | Granted by email to someone without an account yet. Access unlocks when they sign up with that email and verify it. |
| Ended | The subscription was cancelled, access was revoked, or a dispute was opened. |
| Refunded | The payment was fully refunded. |

Each row also shows where the access came from: **Stripe**, **Free claim**, **Added by hand**, or **API**.

## Grant access

Click **Grant access**, pick a product, and paste one email or hundreds. If an email has no account yet, that person gets an invitation (you can turn this off), and their access is waiting when they sign up. People who already have an account in another organization are added to yours as regular members.

You can also grant a product to everyone in a user group at once, for example a whole team or a cohort.

Use this for sales made elsewhere, gifts, team plans and partners. No payment is taken.

## Revoke access

Revoking ends access right away. It doesn't refund a Stripe payment or cancel a Stripe subscription. Do those in Stripe.

## Export

**Export** downloads the current list (with its product and status filters) as a CSV file.

## Buyers' side

Learners see what they bought under **Account → Purchases**. Stripe subscribers can manage their payment method, invoices and cancellation in the Stripe billing portal.

## Stripe statuses

Stripe purchases are kept in sync through webhooks: a paid checkout grants access, a cancelled subscription ends it, a full refund or a dispute revokes it, and a failed payment never revokes access that was already paid for.
