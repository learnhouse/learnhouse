# Products & Pricing

A product is what your learners buy: a price, and the content it unlocks. In the API and webhooks, products are called **offers**.

Under **Payments** → **Products**, click **New product**. Give it a **Name**, a **Description** buyers see on the product page, and optional **Benefits, one per line** (for example "Lifetime access"). Then fill in the sections below and click **Create product**.

<img src="/img/pages/payments/products.webp" alt="The Products tab with product cards showing their checkout type, price, content and number of customers" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

Each product card has **Link** (copy the product's link) and **Grant**.

## What a product can unlock

Pick any mix of your content:

- Courses
- Communities
- Podcasts
- Boards
- Playgrounds

Buyers get access to everything you pick. If someone hasn't bought it yet, the content's page stays visible (its name, description and course outline), and the content itself is locked:

| Content | What stays locked |
|---|---|
| Course | Activities |
| Community | Discussions, comments and reactions |
| Podcast | Episode audio. The episode list stays visible as a preview. |
| Board | The board itself |
| Playground | The playground itself |

Instead of the locked content, people see the products that unlock it. Authors and organization admins always have full access.

## Chapters, lessons and free previews

Inside a course, open a chapter's or lesson's lock menu in the Content tab. Below who can see it (Public, Signed-in only, User groups only), the **Purchase** section sets how it is sold:

- **Included with the course** (default): buyers of the course get it.
- **Free preview**: anyone who can see the course can open it, even when the course is sold. Use it for the first lesson or a sample chapter.
- **Sell this chapter on its own** / **Sell this lesson on its own**: creates a product that unlocks just that chapter or lesson. This also works inside a free course, to sell one premium chapter.

<img src="/img/pages/payments/chapter-purchase-menu.webp" alt="A chapter's lock menu in the course editor, with access options and the Purchase section: Included with the course, Free preview and Sell this chapter on its own" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

Buying the course always opens every chapter and lesson. In the course outline, learners see which lessons are **Paid** and which are **Free previews**, and opening a paid lesson shows the products that unlock it.

## User groups

A product can also add its buyers to one or more user groups (Users → User groups) while their access lasts, under **Also add buyers to user groups**. Buyers then get everything those groups already unlock, including "Users only" content, and the store page lists that content as included.

When access ends, buyers only leave the groups the purchase put them in. People who were in a group before they bought, or who still have another purchase that adds them to it, keep their place. Linking a group to a product adds everyone who already has the product to it.

## How people get it

| Checkout | What happens |
|---|---|
| Stripe Checkout | The buyer pays on a Stripe page. Access unlocks once the payment succeeds. Supports subscriptions, free trials, pay what you want and promotion codes. |
| Your own checkout | The buyer is sent to your link, with `email`, `checkout[email]`, `lh_offer`, `lh_user` and `lh_return` added to it. When the payment clears, your tool calls the [grant API](/platform/payments/api). |
| Free | The learner signs in and claims it. No card needed. |
| Invite only | No buy button. You grant access from Customers or through the API. |

## Pricing

- **One-time** or **subscription** (monthly or yearly).
- **Was (optional)**: a higher price, shown struck through next to the real one.
- **Let buyers choose what to pay** (Stripe, one-time): the price you set is the minimum.
- **Free trial days before the first charge** (Stripe subscriptions).
- **Accept promotion codes at checkout** (Stripe): lets buyers enter a [promotion code](/platform/payments/discounts).

Once a subscription product has active subscribers, its billing period can't be changed. Create a new product for the new period instead.

## Visibility

Products are listed in your store and on the content they unlock. Turn **Show in your store and on its content** off to make a product private: it disappears from the store, but anyone with its link can still open it.

## Deleting a product

You can't delete a product while customers still have access to it. Revoke their access first, or make the product private.
