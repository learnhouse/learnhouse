# Offers & Pricing

An offer is something learners can buy: access to one or more courses, for a one-time price or a subscription.

## Creating an offer

Go to **Payments** → **Offers** and click **Create New Offer**.

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Offer Name** | Required |
| **Description** | Required |
| **Offer Type** | **One Time** or **Subscription** |
| **Price Type** | **Fixed Price** or **Customer Choice**. Customer Choice is only available for one-time offers. |
| **Billing Period** | **Monthly** or **Yearly**. Subscriptions only. |
| **Price** | The amount, at least 1. With Customer Choice this field becomes **Minimum Amount**. |
| **Currency** | Any ISO 4217 currency. USD by default. |
| **Benefits** | Optional selling points, separated by commas. Shown on the store. |
| Access | **Courses (direct)** to unlock individual courses, and/or a **Payment Group** to unlock a bundle |

LearnHouse creates the matching product and price in your Stripe account.

### Customer Choice

With **Customer Choice**, learners pay what they want, starting at your minimum amount. The store labels it "Pay what you want (min.)".

## Managing offers

Each offer card shows its type ("One-time payment" or "Subscription · Monthly/Yearly"), price, benefits and linked payment group.

- **Manage Resources** shows and changes the courses the offer unlocks.
- Edit an offer to change its details. You cannot change the billing period of a subscription that has active subscribers; create a new offer for the new period instead.
- **Archive Offer** removes the offer from LearnHouse and archives its product in Stripe. You cannot archive an offer while it has active or completed enrollments.

<Callout type="info">
Offers are listed on your public [store](/platform/payments/enrollments) and on the pages of the courses they unlock.
</Callout>
