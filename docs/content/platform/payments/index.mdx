# Payments

Sell anything you make on LearnHouse: a single course, a community membership, a podcast, a board, a playground, or a bundle of all of them. Take card payments with Stripe, send buyers to the checkout you already use, give things away, or grant access from your own tools through the API.

<YouTube video="feature-payments" />

<Callout>
Payments is available on the **Standard plan** and above on LearnHouse Cloud. For self-hosted instances, it requires the **Enterprise Edition**.
</Callout>

## How it works

1. **Create a product.** A product is a price and everything it unlocks. See [Products & Pricing](/platform/payments/offers).
2. **Choose how people get it.**
   - **Stripe Checkout**: buyers pay by card, and access unlocks the moment the payment clears.
   - **Your own checkout**: buyers go to your Gumroad, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy or any other page, and your automation grants access through the API.
   - **Free**: one click and it's theirs.
   - **Invite only**: no buy button. You grant access yourself.
3. **Share it.** Every product has its own page in your store, and sold content shows what unlocks it to anyone who hasn't bought it yet.
4. **Manage customers.** Grant, revoke and export access in [Customers & Access](/platform/payments/enrollments), or automate it with [the API](/platform/payments/api).

Only Stripe Checkout needs a connected Stripe account. Every other way of selling works without one.

## The Payments dashboard

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Overview | Revenue, customers, active subscriptions, sales over the last 30 days, and a setup checklist. |
| Products | Create and edit products, copy their links, and grant access. |
| Customers | Everyone with access, however they got it. Search, filter, grant, revoke and export to CSV. |
| Discounts | Stripe promotion codes. |
| Settings | Connect Stripe, and examples for selling with any other tool. |

<img src="/img/pages/payments/payments-overview.webp" alt="The Payments overview with revenue, customers with access, active subscriptions, sales over 30 days and the latest customers" width={1600} height={900} style={{ borderRadius: 10 }} />

**View store** opens your store, and **New product** starts a product from any tab. When Stripe is connected, the overview also shows live Stripe data: MRR, charges and subscriptions.

## Stripe Connect modes

- **Standard**: connect your own Stripe account with OAuth.
- **Express**: LearnHouse creates a Stripe Express account for you, which makes onboarding quicker.
