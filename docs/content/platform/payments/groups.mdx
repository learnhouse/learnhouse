# Groups & Bundles

A payment group bundles several courses so that one offer unlocks them all. It is the way to sell a course bundle or an all-access subscription.

## Creating a group

1. Go to **Payments** → **Payment Groups** and click **New Group**.
2. Enter a **Group Name** (for example "Full Access Bundle" or "Premium Subscription") and an optional description, then click **Create Group**.
3. On the group card, under **Courses**, add the courses the group includes.
4. Link the group to an offer: when you create or edit an offer, pick it under **Payment Group**.

An offer can unlock a payment group and individual courses at the same time.

## Syncing buyers into user groups

Under **Access Groups** on a payment group card, use **Link a UserGroup** to connect one or more [user groups](/platform/users/user-groups).

- When a learner's purchase or subscription becomes active, they are added to every linked user group.
- When the subscription is cancelled or the payment refunded, they are removed again.

This lets a purchase unlock anything a user group can reach (communities, folders, boards, podcasts), not only courses.

## Deleting a group

Deleting a payment group removes its courses and user group links. Offers that used the group stay, but lose their group.
