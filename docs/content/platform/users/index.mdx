# Users and Access

Every person in LearnHouse has one account and can belong to several organizations. Inside each organization they hold one role, which decides what they can see and do there.

<YouTube video="growth-4" />

You manage people from **Users** in the dashboard sidebar. The page has these tabs:

| Tab | What you do there | Cloud plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Users** | Search, filter and export members, change roles, remove people, open learner analytics | All plans |
| **UserGroups** | Group members and give the group access to courses and other content | Standard and above |
| **Roles** | Create custom roles with their own permissions | Pro and above |
| **Signups & Invite Codes** | Choose open or invite-only signup, create invite codes, add custom signup fields | All plans |
| **Invite Members** | Invite people by email | All plans |
| **Sign-in** | Choose which sign-in methods members may use | All plans |
| **Two-factor** | Require two-factor authentication for members | All plans |
| **Audit Logs** | See who did what in the organization | Enterprise |

Self-hosted instances have no plan limits. Audit logs need the [Enterprise Edition](/enterprise).

## Member limits on LearnHouse Cloud

Each plan includes a number of members and admin seats. An admin seat is a member whose role can open the dashboard (Admin, Maintainer and Instructor by default, or a custom role with dashboard access).

| Plan | Members | Admin seats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 10 | 1 |
| Personal | 1 | 1 |
| Personal Family | 4 | 4 |
| Standard | 200 | 2 |
| Pro | 500 | 10 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | 100 |

Pending invitations count toward the member limit. When an organization has more members than its plan includes, the **Users** tab shows a badge such as "3 beyond your plan". Only members active in the current month are counted.
