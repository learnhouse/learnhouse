# Emails

LearnHouse sends email for account and membership events. There is no in-app inbox or notification center: everything below arrives by email.

## Account and membership emails

| Email | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| Account created | Someone signs up |
| Email verification | A new account must confirm its address (LearnHouse Cloud), or the user asks for a new link |
| Magic login link | A member asks to sign in with a [magic link](/platform/users/authentication) |
| Password reset | A member uses **Forgot password?** |
| Invitation | An admin [invites someone by email](/platform/users/members-and-invites#inviting-people-by-email) |
| Joined organization | An existing account becomes a member of another organization. New signups get the account created email instead. |
| Role changed | An admin changes a member's role |
| Email changed | The address on an account is changed. The notice goes to the previous address. |
| Organization created or deleted | You create an organization, or an organization you administer is deleted |
| Account deleted | An account is deleted |

These emails can't be turned off. They carry your organization's branding:

- **Sender name**: the **Email Sender Name** from [organization settings](/platform/organizations/settings#general). The sending address stays the platform's own, so SPF and DKIM keep working. If you leave it empty, emails come from "LearnHouse".
- **Language**: your organization's **Default Language**.
- **Logo and color**: your square logo (or your wide logo if there is no square one) and your primary color from **Branding**.
- **"Powered by LearnHouse"** line: follows your watermark setting on LearnHouse Cloud and the Enterprise Edition. Open source instances always show it.

## Tips for organization admins (LearnHouse Cloud)

On LearnHouse Cloud, organization admins also receive occasional tips about their organization: for example a nudge to publish a course still in draft, a note that a published course has no learners yet, a heads-up when the organization nears a plan limit, or a message to celebrate the first enrollment or completion. Learners never receive these.

- They are sent once a day, at most 2 per week per admin, and at least 48 hours apart.
- Most are skipped when the organization has been active in the last 3 days.
- Self-hosted instances don't send them.

### Unsubscribing

Every tip email has an unsubscribe link. It opens a page that asks you to confirm (**Confirm unsubscribe**), so a mail scanner opening the link can't unsubscribe you by accident. Mail clients that support one-click unsubscribe can also use it directly. Unsubscribing stops these tips only: account emails such as password resets and invitations still arrive.

Addresses that hard-bounce or report a tip as spam stop receiving tips automatically.

<Callout type="info">
Self-hosted instances need an email provider to send any of the emails above. See [Email configuration](/self-hosting/configuration/email).
</Callout>
