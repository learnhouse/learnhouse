# Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a one-time code from an authenticator app every time you sign in. LearnHouse uses standard TOTP codes, so any authenticator app works (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy, and so on). There are no SMS or email codes.

2FA applies to password, magic link and Google sign-ins. After the first step, you have 5 minutes to enter the code.

## Turning on 2FA for your account

<Steps>

### Open your security settings

Go to your account settings and open **Security**. Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Enable**.

### Confirm your password

If your account has a password, enter it and click **Continue**.

### Scan the QR code

Scan the code with your authenticator app. If you cannot scan it, use **Or enter this setup key manually** and **Copy setup key**.

### Enter a code

Type the 6-digit code your app shows into **Authentication code** and click **Verify and enable**.

### Save your backup codes

LearnHouse shows 10 backup codes. This is the only time these codes will be shown, so use **Copy all** or **Download .txt**, tick **I have saved these codes somewhere safe.** and click **Done**.

</Steps>

Each backup code works once, in place of an app code. The Security page shows how many you have left and warns you when 3 or fewer remain. **Regenerate backup codes** replaces the whole set (it asks for a code from your app).

To turn 2FA off, click **Disable** and confirm with your password and an app code or a backup code. This also deletes your backup codes.

<Callout type="warning">
If you lose your phone and your backup codes, an organization admin can reset your 2FA from the **Two-factor** tab (see below).
</Callout>

## Requiring 2FA in your organization

Go to **Users** → **Two-factor** and turn on **Require two-factor authentication**.

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Grace period** | **Immediately**, **7 days**, **14 days** or **30 days**. New members get the same window, counted from the day they join. |
| **Exempt users who sign in via Google or SSO** | On by default. Their identity provider already handles multi-factor. |

When the grace period ends, members without 2FA are blocked from your organization until they enroll. They keep their account and access to any other organization they belong to, and get access back as soon as they set up 2FA. Turning the requirement off restores access immediately.

Saving an active policy again does not restart anyone's countdown. Turning it off and on again does.

### Two-factor coverage

The **Two-factor coverage** panel shows how many members have 2FA, with counts for **Enrolled**, **Exempt (external sign-in)** and **Would be blocked**, and a list of members with members who are not enrolled at the top. Use **Reset 2FA** on a member to clear their second factor, for example after they lose their device.

<Callout type="info">
Only the **Admin** role can change the policy, and you must have 2FA enabled on your own account before you can require it for others.
</Callout>
