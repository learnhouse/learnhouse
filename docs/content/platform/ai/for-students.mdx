# AI for Students

Learners have two AI assistants: **Ask AI** on each activity, and the **Copilot** for questions across courses. Both need to be enabled for the organization (see [AI](/platform/ai#turning-ai-on)), and learners must be signed in.

## Ask AI

On a published activity (any type except assignments), click **Ask AI** to open a chat about the activity you are on. The assistant reads the activity's content, so its answers are about what you are studying.

Suggested questions get you started:

- **What is this Activity about ?**
- **Generate flashcards about this Activity**
- **Explain this Activity in practical examples**

Each message uses one of your organization's AI credits.

## Copilot

The Copilot is an AI assistant for all the courses you can access. Open it from the **Copilot** page (`g` then `a`), from the search palette, or from the Copilot bubble in the organization's menu. It is only available when an admin has turned on **AI Copilot**.

- **Course Only** mode answers from your organization's course material, and cites its sources: click a citation to jump to the lesson it came from.
- **General** mode answers from the AI's general knowledge.
- Ask about **All courses** or pick one course to narrow the answers.
- Conversations are saved. Start a new one with **New Chat**.

The Copilot can draw on course pages, PDFs and documents, assignments and the transcripts of videos and audio. Each message uses 2 AI credits.

## Examples

- **Clarifying concepts**: ask for an explanation of a topic from the current lesson in other words.
- **Summaries**: ask for a summary of a long activity.
- **Practice**: ask for flashcards or practice questions on the material.
- **Finding things**: ask the Copilot which lesson covers a topic.

AI answers can contain mistakes. Check important points against the course material.
