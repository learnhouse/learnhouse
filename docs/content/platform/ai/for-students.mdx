# AI for Students

Learners have two kinds of AI assistant: **Ask AI** on each activity, and **Copilot** and other agents in [Messages](/platform/messages) for questions across courses. Both need to be enabled for the organization (see [AI](/platform/ai#turning-ai-on)), and learners must be signed in.

## Ask AI

On a published activity (any type except assignments), click **Ask AI** to open a chat about the activity you are on. The assistant reads the activity's content, so its answers are about what you are studying.

Suggested questions get you started:

- **What is this Activity about ?**
- **Generate flashcards about this Activity**
- **Explain this Activity in practical examples**

Each message uses one of your organization's AI credits.

## Copilot and agents

Copilot is the built-in AI agent in [Messages](/platform/messages). It answers questions about all the courses you can access, adds its own general knowledge, and cites its sources: click a source to jump to the lesson it came from. Open Messages and pick Copilot from your chats, or open it from the chat dock at the bottom of any page. It is only available when an admin has turned on **AI Copilot**.

You can also build your own agents with their own tone, instructions and courses, mention an agent in a group chat, and ask an agent to remind you about something later. See [Agents](/platform/messages#agents).

Agents can draw on course pages, PDFs and documents, assignments and the transcripts of videos and audio, from courses that have been [indexed for AI](/platform/ai#indexing-courses-for-ai). Each agent reply uses 2 AI credits.

## Examples

- **Clarifying concepts**: ask for an explanation of a topic from the current lesson in other words.
- **Summaries**: ask for a summary of a long activity.
- **Practice**: ask for flashcards or practice questions on the material.
- **Finding things**: ask Copilot which lesson covers a topic.

AI answers can contain mistakes. Check important points against the course material.
