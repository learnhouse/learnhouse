# Demo bundle provenance

Everything in this bundle is original work produced for LearnHouse and is
redistributable under the repository's licence. Nothing here is third-party
content, and nothing depicts a real person or organisation.

## Imagery

All images under `media/` are generated by `apps/api/scripts/build_demo_media.py`
— flat vector compositions drawn with Pillow from a fixed palette defined in
that script. Re-running it reproduces the same bytes.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `media/courses/*.webp` | Course thumbnails. One gradient field plus one geometric motif per course, from a shared six-colour palette. |
| `media/avatars/a01–a24.webp` | Learner avatars. Abstract head-and-shoulders shapes, no facial features. |
| `media/org/logo.webp` | Organisation mark. A shape, not a wordmark. |
| `media/org/thumbnail.webp` | Organisation banner. |

Deliberately **not** used:

- **Photographs of people.** Forty fictional learners with real faces would
  need model releases, and using real photographs for invented people is
  misleading regardless of the licence.
- **AI-generated faces.** Unsettling at small sizes, and the licensing position
  is unsettled.
- **Stock imagery.** Even permissively licensed stock drifts out of visual
  consistency the moment someone adds one more image from a different source.

## Written content

The six courses, their assignments and all persona names are written for this
bundle. The organisation, "Riverbend Supply Co.", and its academy are invented.

The content is deliberately generic — onboarding, writing, support, product
knowledge, working with numbers, and compliance — so that it reads as plausible
to a prospect in any industry without resembling any particular customer's
material.

**No real customer, client or partner may be referenced anywhere in this
bundle**, including in course text, organisation details, persona names,
assignment scenarios or image filenames. `test_demo_bundle.py` enforces this
with a denylist check; extend the denylist rather than relying on review.
